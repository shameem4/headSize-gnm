headSize calibration marker

  1. Print this page at 100% / "Actual size" (turn off "Fit to page"; only the marker and ruler are printed).
  2. Measure the 100 mm line with a real ruler. If it isn't 100 mm, enter what you measured in the demo ("Printed 100 mm line measures"); that corrects for printer scaling.
  3. Glue the marker to stiff card and cut along the dashed line. It must stay flat.
  4. In the demo, click Calibrate with printed marker, take off glasses, hold the marker flat against your forehead, facing the camera, and keep still.
This line must measure exactly 100 mm.
